# Roland MC-303 Notes

Unofficial personal reference notes for the Roland MC-303 Groovebox (1996), written to be read on a phone.

- **[record_cheat.md](./record_cheat.md)**: Button-by-button recording cheat sheet: the Part → Pattern → Song hierarchy, metronome and lead-in, realtime vs step recording, deleting, live LFO/envelope tweaking, a 120 BPM walkthrough for recording one instrument with permanently saved knob automation (Section 5), a deep-house percussion build on the drum Part that adds a new element every 8 bars (Section 6), and copying a pattern, keeping one Part and wiping another, pasting single Parts between patterns and re-recording what you wiped (Section 7).
- **[sample_bank.md](./sample_bank.md)**: Matrix of the MC-303's tone categories and rhythm sets, with a per-bank breakdown of every tone (names and MIDI bank/program numbers).
- **[repair.md](./repair.md)**: Left `SELECT [<]`, `SHIFT` and `OCTAVE [+]` button repair: confirming the faults with the built-in Switch/LED test, workarounds until they're fixed (tap tempo, pedal as TAP or SHIFT, MIDI clock, Pattern Sets, pad octave range), the original tact switch all three buttons share (Panasonic EVQ-21305R / Roland 13169752), a PiShop South Africa shopping list, soldering tips, and the disassembly → desolder → test → reassembly steps.
- **[jonty_first_track.md](./jonty_first_track.md)**: A five-pattern, ~5-minute track (`U41`–`U45`, 160 bars at 124 BPM = 5:10) arranged after Hardrive's "Deep Inside": your hook, a free-style layer and a bass laid down first, then the drums, with every pass listed (pad · steps · bars) in a layout that fits a phone screen, a clock to compare against the record, and the `S-2` song chain.
- **[deep_inside_instructions.md](./deep_inside_instructions.md)**: A reviewed seven-pattern, 202-bar (6:32, 123.6 BPM) MC-303 interpretation of the uploaded *Deep Inside* (`U31`–`U37`, song `S-3`), with compact pass grids, the actual G1/B1 bass register, two-bar/eight-bar phrase changes and broader vocal-proxy coverage. Uses existing recording routines rather than duplicating them.
- **[deep_inside_instructions_v2.md](./deep_inside_instructions_v2.md)**: Start here for the same seven-pattern build as a self-contained, button-by-button walkthrough: empty slots, sounds and lengths, drums recorded first as the rhythm guide, then keys, voice and bass. Every pass has a phone-width pad/octave/bar grid showing hits, chords or bass ties, followed by one-time transposes, timing cleanup and the finished `S-3` Song. No analysis grids or optional extra layers.
- **[raw.md](./raw.md)**: Full stem-analysis review, corrections, confidence limits and reproducibility notes. [`analysis/deep_inside/`](./analysis/deep_inside/) contains the analysis script, regenerated CSV/JSON data, all six DrumSep candidate lanes, quality flags and model/library provenance; no audio is committed.
- **[next_steps.md](./next_steps.md)**: The core features the other notes don't cover, each as a short step-by-step on the finished track: the arpeggiator, effects (reverb, delay, chorus), fixing a take without re-recording (Transpose, Velocity, Gate Time, Shift Clock), editing the Song (Insert Pattern, Song Loop) and performing live (next-pattern queue, Pattern Sets, Variation Patterns, Realtime Transpose).

## Accuracy

- **record_cheat.md** is cross-checked against the text of the official 1996 Owner's Manual and Roland/Sweetwater knowledge-base articles. It has **not** been tested on a physical unit, and display strings marked *(unconfirmed)* are best guesses. Anything described as inferred (for example how shared knobs behave) should be confirmed on your MC-303. The drum patterns in Section 6 are generic deep-house building blocks inspired by Hardrive's "Deep Inside", not a transcription of that record. In Section 7, Part Copy is opened with Pad `2`, which is inferred from the order of the manual's edit functions because the scan doesn't show the number.
- **sample_bank.md**: tone names and MIDI numbers come from the manual's tone list as republished by Planet-Groove and from a community MIDI bank file. The single-hit percussion and drum sections were reconstructed from that community file, so they may contain errors, and a few entries named only by a number are placeholders in the source file.
- The "famous usage" notes in **sample_bank.md** are illustrative. Only Vladislav Delay, :wumpscut: and Redman are documented MC-303 users (via Equipboard), and no famous track is documented against any individual bank.
- **repair.md** is cross-checked against the scanned 1996 Roland MC-303 Service Notes (test mode, parts list, panel schematic and PCB layout), the Owner's Manual and Panasonic's EVQ21305R page. Behaviour those documents don't describe is marked *(unconfirmed)*, for example whether the SELECT indicator wraps around, the generic replacement switch's leg spacing and feel, and the soldering times. PiShop prices and stock were checked on 2026-11-15 and will drift.
- **jonty_first_track.md** takes its facts about the record (the two versions and their lengths, 124 BPM, B minor, the *Beautiful People* vocal) from Discogs, a key/BPM site and an Insomniac feature; the key and tempo are database estimates. No trustworthy bar-by-bar arrangement of the record was available, so the entry bars are a model of a typical early-'90s house 12-inch, not a transcription. The MC-303 only has User Patterns `U01`–`U50`, so patterns "90 onwards" are mapped to `U41` onwards. It has not been tested on a physical unit.
- **deep_inside_instructions.md / raw.md** use machine analysis, not listening or hardware tests. The 2015 remixes-album tag does not prove this is a remix; the unnamed track is consistent with an original reissue. Timing/bass have stronger evidence than instrument identities, chord voicings, vocal pitches or ghost hits. The recipe uses 202 complete bars; the data separately includes the partial tail as analysis bar 203. Neural amplitude is not original MIDI velocity. Factory voice tones cannot reproduce the actual sampled vocal.
- **deep_inside_instructions_v2.md** uses the original recipe's notes and pass ranges with explicit button routines from **record_cheat.md** and **next_steps.md**. It has not been tested on hardware and is still an interpretation, not a verified transcription. It fixes the formerly discretionary keys/voice thinning into repeatable passes, starts with dry sounds and uses tied step entry for bass. Recording drums first and the visual pass grids change the presentation, not the notes or arrangement. The Transpose shortcut and Song Loop page position remain *(unconfirmed)*.
- **next_steps.md** follows the Owner's Manual (pp.14–19, 30–35, 38–41, 48, 65–66, 69–72) and has not been tested on a physical unit. Transpose (Pad `6`), Shift Clock (Pad `9`) and the Song Loop page position are inferred from the manual's order, because the scan doesn't show them clearly. The effect and arpeggio suggestions for the track are by ear.
