## Information available without listening

These are source/repository notes, not claims from hearing the audio:

- Artist/project name is **Hardrive** (often mistyped “Hard Drive”); the repository notes associate the project with Louie Vega.
- The repository identifies the 1993 *Deep Inside* EP on Strictly Rhythm (SR EP2), with an Original listed at 6:28 and a Dub at 5:14.
- The notes use **124 BPM** and **B minor**. They attribute the tempo to Discogs contributors and the key to an automated/key database estimate; neither is printed on the record and neither was checked by listening here.
- The notes say the vocal is sampled from Barbara Tucker’s “Beautiful People.” No lyrics or transcription are recorded here.
- Descriptions in the repository characterize the track as having a deep bassline, recurring piano riff, layered percussion (including claps and hats), and a sampled “deep inside” vocal hook. These are source descriptions, not my own listening observations.
- The linked MC-303 plan is a **model inspired by** the track: five 32-bar patterns at 124 BPM (160 bars, about 5:10), with drum-led intro, bass, piano-like hook, vocal stand-in, break, peak, and outro. The plan explicitly says its bar-by-bar arrangement is not a transcription.
